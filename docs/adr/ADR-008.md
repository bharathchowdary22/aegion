# ADR-008: Vercel for Frontend Deployment

## Decision
We plan to deploy the Next.js frontend application to Vercel.

## Reason
Vercel are the creators of Next.js and provide the most seamless, zero-configuration deployment experience for Next.js applications. It offers automatic CI/CD from GitHub, edge network distribution, and preview deployments out of the box.

## Alternatives considered
- **AWS Amplify**: Good ecosystem integration, but Next.js support can sometimes lag behind Vercel.
- **Render**: Capable of hosting Next.js, but Vercel's edge caching and image optimization are superior for this specific framework.

## Consequences
- Tightly couples frontend deployment to the Vercel ecosystem.
- May face higher costs at significant scale compared to self-hosting, though this is mitigated by the reduced DevOps overhead.
